Welcome to Money Made Simple

Money is something we all use, but most of us don’t learn how to manage it until we’re already making financial decisions. Money Made Simple was created to make those skills easier to understand.

Whether you’re learning how to budget, curious about starting a business, or just want to feel more confident with money, you’re in the right place.

Personal Finance

Budgeting: A budget is a plan for how you will use your money.

Saving: Saving means setting money aside for future needs or goals.

Needs: Things you must pay for, such as transportation or school expenses.

Wants: Things you enjoy but do not necessarily need.

Business Basics

Revenue: The money a business earns from selling products or services.

Expenses: The costs of running a business.

Profit: The money left after expenses are subtracted from revenue.

Profit = Revenue − Expenses
